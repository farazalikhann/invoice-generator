About Invoice Generator

Invoice Generator is a small, focused tool that does one thing: it helps you build a professional invoice and get a PDF out the door, without asking you to create an account, hand over an email address, or wait on a server somewhere to render your document.

Why it's free

Invoicing software usually bundles a simple form with a subscription, a dashboard, and a dozen features most people never touch. This site skips all of that. It's a static page — the form, the live preview, and the PDF export all run as JavaScript in your browser. There's no backend to pay for, so there's no reason to charge for it.

Why there's no signup

An account only makes sense if there's a server keeping track of who you are. This tool doesn't have one. Your business details are saved with your browser's local storage purely so you don't have to retype them next time — that data never travels anywhere. You can clear it at any time with the "Clear Saved Data" button in the tool.

Who it's for

Freelancers, contractors, consultants, and small business owners who need a clean, accurate invoice now — not a full accounting suite. If you eventually outgrow it (multi-user teams, recurring billing automation, client portals), a dedicated invoicing platform is probably the better fit. For everyone else, this is meant to be the fastest way from "I need to bill someone" to a finished PDF.
