Bug reports

If something isn't working, it helps to include your browser and device (for example, "Chrome on Windows 11" or "Safari on iPhone"), what you were trying to do, and what happened instead. Since no invoice data ever leaves your browser, we can't see what you were working on — screenshots are the fastest way to show us.
